Welcome

This is a shared, living world. You are one person or creature in it, and the world goes on around you whether or not you are watching.

Choose a character and describe who you are in your own words: what you do, what brings you, who you know, what matters to you. Everything you state about yourself is remembered forever and shapes your world.

Then write what your character does or says, as you would tell it. A narrator answers with what happens. You may attempt to do anything your character could do here. You cannot decide what others do, only what you do.

Act sends what you wrote. Alone, that is all: the narrator answers with what happens in the world. Other players may be here at the same time. When several are, the world moves in rounds: everyone writes their action and presses Act, and the story goes on when all have. You can see who is in the round and who has acted, never what they wrote. Your action stays in the box; if you type again it is taken back until you press Act again.

Go appears when others are in the round. It closes the round without waiting for everyone: once those who have not acted have stopped typing. Anyone who had not acted does their first suggested action. You can press Go only after you have acted.

Who else is here. When another player or llm controlled bot is online, a line above the box lists them: players online: you · Mira · Tomas. A name in yellow, like Mira, is level with you, in your place at your minute, so you act together. A name in light violet, like Tomas, is elsewhere, in place or in time; hover over it to see where and when. Click a violet name and the page asks, for example, "Join Tomas: advance to 3:40 pm and go to the ferry landing?". Answer Yes and your time advances and you move.

Offline defaults to frozen, when frozen the world cannot affect you. Change it to waiting and your character will respond to the world based on your character's memories.

Time. Each character carries their own present, and an action takes as long as it would, so characters who are apart run at their own pace -- one can ride a day while another waits an hour. If an action would carry you more than half an hour ahead (each world sets its own limit), the page asks first: "You are about to jump ahead ... Proceed? Y / N". N does nothing, and neither does typing a new action instead.

Luck. Each turn a random luck is drawn which affects the outcome of whatever you attempt. It can change the outcome of your actions from providential to ruinous though most of the time it will be in the middle. Ruinous when talking with a good friend might result in mild irritation, ruinous while scaling a cliff is a different matter entirely.

The retire button permanently releases your character to live in the world and they will act on their own.
