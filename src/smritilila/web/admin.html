A bot is an NPC played by an agent: each world offers the living beings richest in memory and the living beings most spoken of, such as a dragon met seldom, and the beings that matter to the world's story, such as its villains and powers (a model reads what each holds, and the names of the whole cast, to say which). A character people play is never offered, but you may add one by name, such as Dunstan. Switching one on makes it playable, logged off and waiting; it is then started by the bot supervisor (scripts/bots_supervisor.py, run on this machine) whenever a person is active in its world, and stopped when the last person leaves.
